About me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Astronomy & Astrophysics at the University of California, Santa Cruz (UCSC). My research is on the formation and evolution of dwarf galaxies in the local universe and on their globular cluster systems, combining deep multi-wavelength imaging with spectroscopy. Before moving to the US, I received my Bachelor's degree in Astronomy at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2021.
